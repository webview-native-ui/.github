# Code of Conduct

We want WebView Native UI to be a welcoming and technically rigorous community.

Be constructive and respectful. Debate ideas and implementations rather than attacking people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, personal insults, and sharing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation where necessary.

To report misconduct, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method published in the affected repository. Avoid disclosing sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly where practical, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
